# Visitor visas

Visitor visas are nonimmigrant visas for people who want to enter the United States temporarily for business, tourism, or a combination of both purposes. A visa permits a traveler to seek admission at a U.S. port of entry; it does not guarantee entry.

## Application overview

Applicants generally complete the online nonimmigrant visa application, pay the applicable fee, and schedule an interview when required. Required evidence and processing steps can vary by embassy or consulate and by the applicant's circumstances. Travelers should consult the linked official source and the website of the embassy or consulate where they will apply for current instructions.

## Admission

U.S. Customs and Border Protection decides whether an arriving traveler may enter and how long they may remain. Travelers should confirm that their passport and visa details are accurate and review the admission record after arrival.
