# Transit through the United States

Travelers connecting through a U.S. airport normally undergo U.S. immigration and customs processing because many airports do not provide sterile international transit. They must have documentation that permits them to seek admission or transit, even when the United States is not their final destination.

## Arrival process

On arrival, travelers present their travel documents to Customs and Border Protection. They may need to collect checked baggage, complete customs processing, and recheck baggage for the onward flight. Connection procedures vary by airport and itinerary, so travelers should confirm details with their airline and allow sufficient connection time.

## Current requirements

Transit visa and Visa Waiver Program eligibility depend on the traveler and itinerary. Travelers should use the linked official source and Department of State guidance to verify current requirements before travel.
