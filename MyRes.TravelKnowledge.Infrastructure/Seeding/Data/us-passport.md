# Passport requirements

International travelers must carry a passport that meets the requirements for their route and admission category. Visa Waiver Program travelers generally need an e-passport and an approved Electronic System for Travel Authorization before travel.

## Validity and traveler details

Passport-validity rules can depend on nationality and applicable agreements. The traveler's reservation, passport, visa, and authorization details should be consistent. Travelers should check current official requirements before departure because eligibility and document rules can change.

## Visa Waiver Program

An approved ESTA is a travel authorization, not a guarantee of admission. Customs and Border Protection makes the admission decision at the port of entry. A traveler who is not eligible for visa-free travel must obtain the appropriate visa before departure.
